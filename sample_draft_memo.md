# LISC NY - Draft Deal Memo
**Date:** May 13, 2026
**To:** LISC NY Credit Committee
**From:** Lawrence (Underwriter)
**Subject:** Proposed Financing for "The Brooklyn Heights Affordable Initiative"

## 1. Project Overview
**Project Name:** The Brooklyn Heights Affordable Initiative
**Developer Name:** Apex Community Builders LLC
**Location:** Brooklyn, NY (Kings County)
**Loan Type:** New Construction
**Target Agency:** NYS HCR (New Construction Capital Program - NCP)

## 2. Project Details
The Brooklyn Heights Affordable Initiative is a proposed new construction project that will transform a vacant lot into a mid-rise affordable housing complex. The project aims to serve low-to-moderate-income families in a high-opportunity neighborhood.

**Total Units:** 50
**Target AMI:** 60% AMI across all units.
**Construction Timeline:** Estimated 18 months.

## 3. Financial Request
The developer is requesting capital to cover hard costs and site acquisition.
**Requested Loan Amount:** $8,000,000
**Per Unit Subsidy Request:** $160,000 per affordable housing unit.

## 4. Regulatory & Compliance
The developer has agreed to enter into a regulatory agreement to ensure long-term affordability.
**Proposed Regulatory Agreement Term:** 30 years.

## 5. Developer Experience
Apex Community Builders LLC has successfully completed three similar projects in the Bronx over the last decade. They are in good standing and have no existing defaults on mortgage financing.
